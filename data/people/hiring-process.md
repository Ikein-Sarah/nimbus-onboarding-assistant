# Hiring Process (Internal)

Last updated by: tunde@nimbuslabs.io

## Stages

1. Screen (recruiter or hiring manager, 30 min)
2. Take-home or pairing exercise (role-dependent)
3. Panel interview
4. Final conversation with the hiring manager

## Scorecards

Every interviewer submits a scorecard within 24 hours, before seeing
other interviewers' notes. Scorecards are stored in the applicant
tracking system (ATS, see `general/glossary.md`) and are not shared with
the candidate.

## Panel rules

A panel has at least three people and at least one person outside the
hiring team. No single interviewer can block or force a hire — a strong
no from one panelist is a serious flag but not an automatic reject, same
as a strong yes from one isn't an automatic hire.

## Offers

Offers are drafted by the People team against the approved salary band
for the role and level (see `salary-bands.md`). Offers above band
midpoint require Finance sign-off — this is the same sign-off boundary
referenced from the opposite direction in `priya@nimbuslabs.io`'s and
`amaka@nimbuslabs.io`'s review notes, where above-band placements during
a review cycle also route through Finance.

## Rejected candidates

Candidate records are kept for 12 months, then deleted, per the general
retention principle in `general/security-policy.md` section 3.3.

## New hire handoff to onboarding

Once an offer is signed, People kicks off the checklist in
`onboarding-checklist.md` (the "What People runs for every new joiner"
section) — IT provisioning should start at least 3 working days before
the confirmed start date.
