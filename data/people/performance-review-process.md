# Performance Review Process (Internal)

Last updated by: tunde@nimbuslabs.io

## Cycle

Reviews run twice a year, in March and September. New joiners are
reviewed in the first full cycle after their 90th day.

**This section is out of date** — `performance-review-cycle-2026.md`
adds quarterly check-ins between the two formal cycles below, effective
2026-07-01. The two formal review months themselves (March, September)
are unchanged.

## Inputs

Self review, manager review, and two peer reviews. Peer reviewers are
chosen by the employee and confirmed by the manager.

## Ratings

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Developing | Below expectations for level, active support plan |
| Solid | Meeting expectations for level |
| Strong | Exceeding expectations for level |
| Exceptional | Significantly exceeding, rare |

Ratings are calibrated across each department before release so managers
apply them consistently — see `salary-bands.md`'s "Review" section for
how calibration interacts with band placement.

## Link to pay

Calibrated ratings feed the annual compensation review. The mapping from
rating to increase is set by Finance and People together and is not
published to all staff.

## Confidentiality

Review notes are visible to the employee, their manager, and the People
team only. Real examples of this document type live in individual
personal folders — e.g. `personal/sarah@nimbuslabs.io/review-notes-2026-09.md`
— not in this policy document, which only describes the process.
