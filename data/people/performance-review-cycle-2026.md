# Performance Review Cycle 2026 (Revised)

> Supersedes `performance-review-process.md` (effective 2026-01-10). The
> ratings scale, confidentiality rule, and link-to-pay mechanism are
> unchanged and not repeated in full — this doc only covers what's
> different: cadence and inputs.

Last updated by: tunde@nimbuslabs.io

## Why this changed

Feedback from the last two cycles (via the internal engagement survey,
not published) was consistent: six months is too long to go without
structured feedback, especially for people in their first year. Formal
calibrated ratings twice a year are staying — they're tied to
compensation and calibration takes real coordination — but we're adding
lighter check-ins in between.

## The new cadence

| Touchpoint | When | Formal rating? |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 check-in | Late February | No |
| **Formal review** | March | Yes — calibrated |
| Q3 check-in | Late August | No |
| **Formal review** | September | Yes — calibrated |

The two formal review months (March and September) are unchanged from the
old process. What's new is the check-ins in between.

## Check-ins vs. formal reviews

Check-ins are a 30-minute conversation between you and your manager. No
self-review form, no peer reviews, no calibration, and nothing is written
into your permanent record beyond brief manager notes. The point is
course-correction, not evaluation.

Formal reviews keep the full old process: self review, manager review,
and two peer reviews chosen by the employee and confirmed by the manager,
then calibrated across the department before release.

## New joiners

Same rule as before: new joiners are reviewed in the first full formal
cycle after their 90th day. If your 90th day falls between formal cycles,
you'll get a check-in in the meantime even though you're not yet due a
formal rating — this is new, under the old process there was nothing
scheduled for you until your first formal review.

## Everything else

Ratings (developing, solid, strong, exceptional), the link to
compensation, and confidentiality (visible to employee, manager, and
People only) are unchanged from `performance-review-process.md`. Refer to
that document for those specifics if this page doesn't repeat them —
don't assume "not mentioned here" means "no longer true."

## A note on the personal record

Individual review notes live in each employee's personal folder, not in
this document or anywhere department-wide. This document describes the
process; it does not contain anyone's actual review content.
