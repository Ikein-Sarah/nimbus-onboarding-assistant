# Benefits Overview

Last updated by: tunde@nimbuslabs.io

## Health cover

Health insurance is provided through a local plan in your country of
employment — the specifics vary by country, so this doc can't give exact
numbers. Ask People for your country's plan summary if you haven't
received one; it should have gone out with your offer paperwork.

## Leave

Covered in full in `general/leave-policy.md`, not repeated here. This doc
exists for the benefits that aren't leave.

## Equipment stipend

The 400 USD one-time home office stipend is described in
`general/hybrid-work-policy.md` and claimed via
`finance/expense-policy.md`. Listed here too because "benefits" is where
people instinctively look for it.

## Learning and development

Each employee has an annual 500 USD learning budget — courses, books,
conference tickets. Unlike the home office stipend, this **does** roll
over, up to one year (so in year two, unused budget could total up to
1,000 USD, but not beyond that). Submitted through the same expense
process as everything else, tagged "learning and development."

## Parental leave

Covered in `general/leave-policy.md` — restated here only because it's
genuinely a benefit, not just a leave category: 16 weeks paid for primary
caregivers, 6 weeks for secondary caregivers.

## What we don't offer

To be direct about this, since it comes up often in candidate
conversations and occasionally from current employees who assumed
otherwise:

- No stock options or equity program.
- No company pension or retirement matching beyond whatever your country
  legally requires through payroll.
- No dedicated wellness stipend separate from the learning budget above.

See `general/faq.md` for the informal version of this same answer — this
section exists here too because people search "benefits" more often than
they search "FAQ."

## Questions

Country-specific benefit questions go to your People Partner directly
(Tunde, for the current demo roster) rather than a Slack channel, since
the details aren't uniform enough to answer generically.
