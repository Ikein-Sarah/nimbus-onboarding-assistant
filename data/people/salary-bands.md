# Salary Bands 2026 (Internal, People team)

Last updated by: tunde@nimbuslabs.io

These bands are used to draft offers and to review compensation. They
are not published to all staff — see `general/faq.md`'s "Can I see
someone else's salary" entry for the informal version of this boundary.

## Engineering (annual, USD)

| Level | Range |
|---|---|
| E1 Junior | 38,000 - 52,000 |
| E2 Mid | 52,000 - 74,000 |
| E3 Senior | 74,000 - 98,000 |
| E4 Staff | 98,000 - 126,000 |

## Growth (annual, USD)

| Level | Range |
|---|---|
| G1 | 32,000 - 46,000 |
| G2 | 46,000 - 66,000 |
| G3 | 66,000 - 88,000 |

## People and Finance (annual, USD)

| Level | Range |
|---|---|
| P1/F1 | 30,000 - 44,000 |
| P2/F2 | 44,000 - 62,000 |
| P3/F3 | 62,000 - 84,000 |

## Review

Bands are reviewed each January against market data. Individual
placement within a band depends on level, location and calibrated
performance rating.

## Offer drafting note

`hiring-process.md`'s offer step should always draft against the current
January version of this document. Offers drafted in the days right
before a January band refresh have occasionally referenced the outgoing
year's numbers by mistake — see the note in
`people/onboarding-checklist.md`'s "Common gaps" section.
