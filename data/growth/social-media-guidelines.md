# Social Media Guidelines

Last updated by: amaka@nimbuslabs.io

Hi! This one's a bit more casual than most of the Growth docs, since
social is a more casual channel — but a few of these rules are hard
rules, not vibes, and those are marked.

## Official accounts

Nimbus Labs has company accounts on LinkedIn and X. Posting access is
limited to Growth team members who've been added by Priya. If you
have an idea for a post but not access, drop it in #growth-social and
someone will pick it up.

## Voice on social

Same core voice as everywhere else — see `brand-guidelines.md` — but
social can be a little warmer and more first-person than product copy.
Still no exclamation-mark spam, still no unverifiable performance claims.

## Employee advocacy (this means you, not just Growth)

Anyone at Nimbus Labs can post about work on their personal accounts —
we encourage it. A few hard rules apply here, not just to official
accounts:

- **Hard rule:** never share unreleased product details, screenshots of
  internal tools, or anything from a restricted document folder. This is
  the same confidentiality boundary as `general/code-of-conduct.md`, just
  applied to a public platform instead of an external conversation.
- **Hard rule:** don't share specific customer names or logos unless
  Growth has confirmed written permission exists (same rule as
  `brand-guidelines.md`'s claims section).
- Soft guidance: if you're not sure whether something's fine to post,
  it's genuinely fine to just ask in #growth-social. Nobody's going to be
  annoyed at the question.

## Responding to comments

Official accounts: Growth team handles responses during working hours.
If something looks like a real customer complaint rather than a troll,
loop in the account's actual owner in the CRM (per `crm-rules.md`'s
ownership rule) rather than responding generically.

## Crisis / bad press situations

If something is going publicly badly — a viral complaint, a bug getting
attention, anything reputational — do not respond from the official
account without Priya's sign-off first, even if you think you know
the right answer. This is the one place where "just post it, we'll fix
it later" is explicitly the wrong call. Loop in the CEO if it looks
serious; Growth doesn't have to carry that alone.

## Metrics

We track engagement loosely but don't chase vanity metrics. If a post
underperforms, that's fine — see the campaign measurement philosophy in
`campaign-process.md` ("report the chosen metric even when it looks
bad"), same spirit applies here even though social isn't run through the
formal campaign process.
