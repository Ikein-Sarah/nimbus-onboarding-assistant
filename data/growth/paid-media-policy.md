# Paid Media Policy

Last updated by: priya@nimbuslabs.io

This sits between `campaign-process.md` (the general campaign workflow)
and `finance/expense-policy.md` (general spending rules). Paid media has
its own doc because ad spend behaves differently from a normal expense —
it's recurring, it can scale up automatically if you're not careful, and
the platforms make it easy to overspend without anyone approving each
individual charge.

## Approval threshold is lower than general campaigns

`campaign-process.md` sets Finance approval at 5,000 USD for a campaign.
For paid media specifically, the threshold is **2,000 USD per month, per
channel**, not per campaign. This is intentionally stricter, because a
single campaign can spread across several paid channels (search, social,
retargeting) and each one individually staying under 5,000 USD can still
add up to a large total spend that nobody explicitly approved as a whole.

If your total paid media spend across all channels for a campaign will
exceed 2,000 USD/month on any single channel, get Finance approval before
launch, even if the campaign's total budget is under the general 5,000
USD threshold in `campaign-process.md`. Yes, this means some campaigns
need Finance approval under this doc that wouldn't under the general one
— that's intentional, not a mistake.

## Daily spend caps

Every paid campaign must have a daily spend cap set at the platform level,
not just a total budget. Platforms will happily spend an entire monthly
budget in a single day if something goes wrong (bad targeting, a bug in
an automated bidding rule) — a daily cap limits the damage while someone
notices and reacts.

## Kill switch

Every paid campaign needs one person (not necessarily the campaign owner)
who can pause it within 30 minutes during business hours if asked. Name
this person in the campaign brief. This is new as of this policy — older
brief templates in `campaign-process.md`'s launch checklist don't
explicitly ask for it yet, add it anyway.

## Reconciliation

Paid media invoices are reconciled monthly against the platform's own
reporting by Finance, not by Growth. If Growth's dashboard numbers and
the invoice don't match, that's expected to be flagged during
reconciliation — Growth doesn't need to manually cross-check every
invoice, but should be ready to explain spend if asked.

## Relationship to the expense policy

Paid media spend is **not** submitted through the normal expense process
in `finance/expense-policy.md` — it goes through direct platform billing
set up by Finance. Don't try to expense an ad platform charge as a
personal reimbursement; if you paid for something on a personal card by
mistake, talk to Finance directly rather than filing a standard expense
claim, because the accounting treatment is different.
