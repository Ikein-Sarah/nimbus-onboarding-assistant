# Campaign Planning Process

Last updated by: amaka@nimbuslabs.io

## Proposing a campaign

Write a one-page brief with the audience, the offer, the channels, the
budget and the single metric you will judge it on. Submit it in the
Growth planning board.

New joiners: your first brief should also be reviewed by your manager
before submission, per `onboarding-checklist.md`. That's a
new-joiner-specific step, not something everyone does.

## Approval

Campaigns under 5,000 USD are approved by the Growth lead. Anything above
that also needs Finance approval and follows the procurement rules in
`finance/procurement-approvals.md`.

**If the campaign includes paid media** (ads on any platform), also check
`paid-media-policy.md` — it sets a stricter 2,000 USD/month per-channel
threshold that can require Finance approval even when the campaign's
total stays under the 5,000 USD line above. This is the single most
common approval mistake new Growth hires make, per
`finance/onboarding-checklist.md`'s notes for new Finance hires reviewing
these requests.

## Launch checklist

- [ ] Tracking links in place
- [ ] Landing page reviewed
- [ ] Brand check done, per `brand-guidelines.md`
- [ ] Rollback plan for paid spend
- [ ] Kill-switch owner named, if the campaign includes paid media (see
      `paid-media-policy.md` — this item isn't in the original checklist
      format but is now required for any paid component)

## Measurement

Report results 14 days after the campaign ends. Report the chosen metric
even when it looks bad — this "say the number, even when it's bad" line
gets quoted company-wide, see `general/employee-handbook.md`'s values
section. Campaigns that miss the target twice are stopped, not doubled
down on.

## A note on scheduling

Launches sometimes need meetings outside the normal no-meeting block
described in `general/meeting-norms.md` (Wednesday afternoons). That's
fine for launch week specifically — just don't make it a habit outside
launch periods.
