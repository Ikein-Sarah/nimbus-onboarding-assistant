# Brand and Voice Guidelines

Last updated by: priya@nimbuslabs.io

## Voice

Plain, specific and calm. We describe what the product does and skip
superlatives. No exclamation marks in product copy. This same "plain over
clever" instinct is called out as a company value in
`general/employee-handbook.md` — it's not just a marketing preference,
it's supposed to show up in internal docs too (with mixed success, see
this very document's TODO at the bottom).

### Examples

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "Revolutionary workflow automation!" | "Automates dispatch scheduling for logistics teams." |
| "Loved by customers everywhere" | A specific, sourced customer quote, or nothing |
| "Best-in-class support" | What the support actually includes |

## Naming

The company is written as Nimbus Labs, never NimbusLabs or Nimbus.
The product is written as Nimbus Flow on first mention, then Flow.

## Claims

Every performance claim needs a source, and the source must be less than
12 months old. Customer names may only be used with written permission
from the customer, held by the Growth team — this is the same permission
requirement referenced in `social-media-guidelines.md`, just stated there
for a different channel.

## Visual basics

Primary colour is deep blue. Logo needs clear space equal to the height
of the mark on all sides. Never rotate or recolour the logo.

## Where this applies

This governs anything customer-facing: the website, product copy, sales
decks, and social posts (`social-media-guidelines.md`). It does not
govern internal docs — this doc, for instance, uses more sections and
tables than the "plain and specific" voice guidance would probably want
in a customer-facing piece, which is a fair criticism if you want to make
it.
