# Hybrid Work Policy

> This document supersedes `remote-work-policy.md` (effective 2026-02-01).
> That document is kept for historical reference only — if you find a copy
> of it linked from an old Notion page or a bookmark, this one wins.

Last updated by: tunde@nimbuslabs.io

## Why this changed

The all-remote model worked well up to about 50 people. Past that, several
teams told People ops that async-only coordination was slowing down
cross-department work, especially between Engineering and Growth during
launches. This policy keeps Nimbus Labs remote-friendly but introduces a
lightweight anchor day for teams that share a city.

## Default arrangement

- Nimbus Labs remains remote-first. There is still no company-wide required
  number of office days.
- Teams with three or more people in the same metro area agree one shared
  "anchor day" per week with their manager. This is a team decision, not a
  company mandate — see `meeting-norms.md` for how anchor days interact with
  meeting scheduling.
- Fully distributed teams (no city with 3+ people) are unaffected and keep
  the old fully-remote arrangement.

## Working from another country

Unchanged from the prior policy, restated here for completeness:

- Up to 30 days per year without approval.
- Beyond 30 days, written approval from the People team is required, because
  tax and employment rules may change. Submit the request at least 3 weeks
  before travel through the People Slack channel.

## Equipment

- New joiners get a laptop and a one-time home office stipend of 400 USD,
  claimed through the expense process (`finance/expense-policy.md`) within
  their first 90 days.
- Anchor-day teams may request a shared desk booking budget from their
  department lead; this comes out of the department's own envelope per
  `finance/budget-planning.md`, not a separate company fund.

## Team gatherings

Each team still meets in person twice a year. Travel is booked through the
Finance team and follows the expense policy. Anchor days do **not** replace
these twice-yearly gatherings — they're a different thing (weekly, local,
optional attendance) versus the gathering (twice yearly, company-funded
travel, expected attendance).

## Open question

Whether contractors are covered by the anchor-day clause is still being
worked out with Legal. Until that's resolved, treat contractors as exempt.
