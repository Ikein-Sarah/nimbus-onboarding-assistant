# Meeting Norms

Last updated by: priya@nimbuslabs.io — honestly this page exists because
Growth was getting double-booked constantly and someone had to write
something down. Other departments, feel free to adapt.

## The basics

- Default meeting length is 25 or 50 minutes, not 30 or 60. Calendar
  defaults are wrong, please change yours. This gives everyone a buffer
  between back-to-back meetings.
- No meeting should exist without an agenda in the invite. If you can't
  write one sentence about what the meeting is for, it's probably an
  async Slack message instead.
- Default to declining a meeting with no agenda. This is explicitly okay
  and you won't get in trouble for it.

## Core hours and time zones

Per `employee-handbook.md`, core hours are 10:00-16:00 in your local time
zone. Meetings that cross time zones should land inside the overlap of
everyone's core hours — if there's no overlap, it should probably be async
instead of a meeting nobody can attend awake.

## Recording

Recurring meetings with more than 5 people are recorded by default so
people who couldn't make it can catch up. 1:1s and anything discussing an
individual's performance are **never** recorded, no exceptions.

## Anchor days

Since `hybrid-work-policy.md` introduced anchor days for co-located teams,
a few norms have settled in:

- Don't schedule remote-only meetings during someone else's team's anchor
  day if you can help it — that's the one day they're optimizing for
  in-person time.
- Anchor days are not mandatory company-wide, so don't assume everyone in
  a video call knows what "our anchor day" means. Some teams don't have
  one.

## Skipping meetings

You can skip any recurring meeting if:
- You have nothing to contribute or gain that week, and
- You leave an async update if one was expected of you.

Standing 1:1s with your manager are the exception — reschedule, don't
skip, unless your manager says otherwise.

## No-meeting blocks

Wednesdays after 14:00 (your local time) are protected as a
no-internal-meeting block, company-wide. Customer calls are exempt. This
has been in place since mid-2025 and mostly holds, except around launches
— see the honest note in `growth/campaign-process.md`'s launch checklist,
which doesn't mention this and probably should.

## FAQ-ish notes

**Can I just not have a camera on?** Yes. Nobody enforces camera-on.

**What if my manager double-books me?** Tell them. This document isn't
a substitute for just talking to people.
