# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Default arrangement
Nimbus Labs is remote first. There is no required number of office days.
Teams that share a city may agree their own in-person rhythm.

## Working from another country
You may work from another country for up to 30 days per year without approval.
Beyond 30 days you need written approval from the People team, because tax and
employment rules may change.

## Equipment
New joiners get a laptop and a one-time home office stipend of 400 USD.
Claim the stipend through the expense process in your first 90 days.

## Team gatherings
Each team meets in person twice a year. Travel is booked through the Finance
team and follows the expense policy.
