# Leave and Time Off Policy

Last updated by: tunde@nimbuslabs.io

This is the detailed leave policy. `employee-handbook.md` also mentions
leave briefly in passing ("Who to ask") — this document is the
authoritative one for actual entitlements and request timing; the
handbook just points you here.

## Annual leave

All full-time employees get 24 working days of paid annual leave per
year, accrued monthly (2 days per completed month). Up to 5 unused days
carry over into the next year and expire on 31 March.

| Leave length | Notice required | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 days | 2 working days | Your manager |
| 3+ days | 10 working days | Your manager |
| Extended (2+ weeks) | 4 weeks, discuss with manager early | Your manager, People CC'd |

## Requesting leave

Submit requests in Workday at least 10 working days in advance for leave
of 3 days or more, and at least 2 working days in advance for shorter
leave. Your manager approves or declines within 3 working days. If your
manager doesn't respond in that window, escalate one level up per
`org-chart.md` rather than assuming silence means approval — it doesn't.

## Sick leave

Up to 10 paid sick days per year, no medical note required for absences
of 2 days or fewer. Notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day.
For absences longer than 2 days, a medical note may be requested by
People, not by your manager directly.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers get 16 weeks paid. Secondary caregivers get 6 weeks
paid. Notify the People team at least 8 weeks before your expected start
date. This is separate from — and does not draw down — your annual leave
balance.

## Public holidays

You follow the public holiday calendar of the country you are employed
in. Public holidays do not count against annual leave.

## Interaction with working from abroad

If you're on leave and also working from another country per
`hybrid-work-policy.md`, the leave itself doesn't need separate approval
from the country-abroad rule — they're independent. Being on leave
somewhere else for under 30 days needs nothing extra; leave doesn't reset
or extend the 30-day counter either way.
