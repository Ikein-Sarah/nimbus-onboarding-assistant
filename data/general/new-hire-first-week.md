# New Hire First Week Checklist

Last updated by: tunde@nimbuslabs.io

This is the company-wide version. Your department has its own onboarding
checklist with role-specific steps — see `engineering/onboarding-checklist.md`,
`growth/onboarding-checklist.md`, `people/onboarding-checklist.md`, or
`finance/onboarding-checklist.md`. Do both; they cover different things.

Note: this is a more detailed version of the "Your first week" section in
`employee-handbook.md`. If the two ever disagree on timing, follow this one
— the handbook section is a summary and doesn't get updated as often.

## Before day 1

- [ ] People team sends your offer letter and start date confirmation
- [ ] IT provisions your email, Slack, Workday and Google Workspace accounts
- [ ] Your manager picks an onboarding buddy and tells them before you start

## Day 1

- [ ] Laptop pickup or courier delivery confirmed
- [ ] Set up 1Password, enroll 2FA on every account (see `it-setup.md`)
- [ ] Welcome call with your manager — 30 minutes, expectations and first
      two weeks
- [ ] Join #general, #random, and your department Slack channel
- [ ] Read `code-of-conduct.md` and `employee-handbook.md`

## Day 2-3

- [ ] Read your department's onboarding checklist
- [ ] Meet your onboarding buddy
- [ ] Meet your immediate team (async intros are fine if timezones don't
      line up)
- [ ] Confirm your Workday profile is correct — legal name, emergency
      contact, bank details for payroll

## Day 4-5

- [ ] Pair with your buddy on a small starter task
- [ ] Book your 30-day check-in with your manager (People team sends a
      reminder if you don't)
- [ ] Skim `org-chart.md` so you know who's who

## By end of week 2

- [ ] Complete the security awareness read of `security-policy.md`
- [ ] If eligible, confirm you're queued for the next onboarding cohort call
      run by People (monthly, all new joiners from that month)

## Common gaps we see

People team flagged these as the most frequently missed items in the last
two onboarding retros:

1. New joiners forget to claim the 400 USD home office stipend within the
   90-day window (`finance/expense-policy.md`) — it does not roll over.
2. Engineering joiners sometimes skip 2FA enrollment on the cloud console
   specifically, only doing email/Slack.
3. Nobody reads `meeting-norms.md` until they've already double-booked
   someone's focus block. Read it in week 1, not week 4.
