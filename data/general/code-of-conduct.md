# Code of Conduct

Last updated by: tunde@nimbuslabs.io

## Our standards

Treat colleagues, customers and candidates with respect. Assume good intent,
disagree in the open, and commit once a decision is made. This applies in
Slack, in meetings, in code review comments, and on social media when
you're identifiable as a Nimbus Labs employee (see
`growth/social-media-guidelines.md` for the specifics on that last one).

### Disagreeing well

"Disagree in the open" means in the shared channel or the PR thread, not
in a DM afterward to someone else about the person you disagreed with.
If a disagreement can't resolve in two rounds of back-and-forth, escalate
to whoever owns the decision rather than letting it go quiet unresolved —
`engineering/code-review-guidelines.md` has a concrete version of this for
code reviews specifically.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment of any kind, including in DMs, on social platforms, or at
  company gatherings.
- Sharing confidential company or customer data outside Nimbus Labs.
- Misrepresenting your work or the company's capabilities — this includes
  overstating a feature to a customer or overstating your own contribution
  in a performance review self-assessment.
- Retaliating against someone for raising a concern in good faith.

## Confidentiality

Customer data, unreleased product plans, and other employees' personal
records are confidential. Do not copy them to personal devices or personal
accounts. This is the same boundary described more mechanically in
`general/security-policy.md` (folder-level access control) and
`employee-handbook.md`'s "Document access" section — this document is the
values statement, those are the how-it-works and how-it's-enforced
versions.

## Reporting a concern

Raise concerns with your manager, or with the People team if your manager
is involved. Reports are handled by the People team and are kept
confidential where possible — "where possible" because in some cases
(e.g. a concern involving physical safety) confidentiality has to give way
to acting quickly.

| If the concern involves... | Report to |
|---|---|
| A peer or someone outside your reporting line | Your manager |
| Your manager | People team directly |
| A security or data issue rather than a behavioral one | `general/security-policy.md` section 5 instead — different process |

## Consequences

Breaches are reviewed by the People team and may lead to a written warning,
removal of system access, or termination depending on severity. There is
no fixed escalation ladder published here deliberately — severity and
context both matter, and a rigid point system tends to produce worse
outcomes than People judgment applied consistently.
