# Invoicing and Billing FAQ

Last updated by: lara@nimbuslabs.io. Written the same way
`general/faq.md` is — casual, because most of these questions come in
casually over Slack and deserve a casual answer, not a policy memo.

## "A vendor sent an invoice, what do I do with it?"

Forward it to #finance-invoices with the vendor name and what it's for.
If the vendor isn't already set up (see
`vendor-payment-runbook.md`), that has to happen before payment, so flag
that up front if you know it's a new vendor.

## "Can I just pay this small thing with my own card and expense it?"

For genuinely small, one-off things under the no-pre-approval limits in
`expense-policy.md`, sure. For anything recurring — a subscription, a
retainer — please don't, even if it's small. Recurring charges on
personal cards are a nightmare to track and reconcile, and they skip the
vendor setup process entirely, which means no one but you knows the
charge exists if you leave or forget about it.

## "Why did my expense reimbursement take so long?"

Reimbursements go out in the next payroll run, per `expense-policy.md`.
If you submitted right after a payroll run closed, that can mean waiting
almost a full cycle. This is annoying and we know it — there isn't a
faster path right now for standard reimbursements.

## "What's the difference between a campaign budget approval and a paid media approval?"

Genuinely confusing, worth its own answer here even though it's a Growth
process question. `growth/campaign-process.md` sets the general campaign
approval threshold at 5,000 USD. `growth/paid-media-policy.md` sets a
stricter threshold specifically for ad spend: 2,000 USD/month per
channel. A campaign can be under the first threshold and still trigger
the second. If you're reviewing a Growth spend request, check which kind
it actually is.

## "Do we have a company credit card I can use?"

Not a general one, no. Some roles have an individual company card for
recurring work spend — that's arranged individually with Henrik, not a
default benefit.

## "Someone asked me for our banking details over email, is that legit?"

Almost certainly not. Legitimate banking detail changes for Nimbus Labs
never come through an unsolicited email. Treat this as a potential
phishing/fraud attempt and report it per `general/security-policy.md`
section 5, don't just quietly ignore it.

## "What's an 'envelope'?"

A department's spending allocation for the year — see `glossary.md` in
the general folder, or `budget-planning.md` for the full mechanics.
