# Expense Policy

Last updated by: henrik@nimbuslabs.io

## General rule

Spend company money as if it were your own, and always attach a receipt.

## Limits without pre-approval

| Category | Limit |
|---|---|
| Software | 50 USD/month |
| Meals | 40 USD/person |
| Taxis | 30 USD/trip |

Anything above these limits should be discussed with your manager
informally before submitting, not necessarily formally pre-approved in a
system — see `finance/invoicing-and-billing-faq.md` for the informal
version of this guidance.

## Travel

Economy flights only for trips under 6 hours. Hotel cap is 180 USD per
night, or 240 USD in London, New York and Singapore.

## Receipts

Submit receipts within 30 days. Claims older than 60 days are not
reimbursed.

## Reimbursement

Approved claims are paid in the next payroll run. If you submit right
after a payroll run closes, this can feel like a long wait — see the FAQ
entry in `invoicing-and-billing-faq.md` acknowledging this directly.

## Home office stipend

The 400 USD new joiner stipend is claimed here, tagged as "home office" —
not "equipment," which is a common miscategorization flagged in
`finance/onboarding-checklist.md`. Claim within your first 90 days per
`general/hybrid-work-policy.md`; it does not roll over.

## What does NOT go through this process

Paid media / ad spend is billed directly by Finance per
`growth/paid-media-policy.md` and should not be submitted as a personal
expense claim, even if you paid for it personally by accident — talk to
Finance directly in that case instead.
