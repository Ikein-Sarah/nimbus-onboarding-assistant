# Procurement and Approval Matrix

Last updated by: henrik@nimbuslabs.io

## Approval levels (annual contract value, USD)

| Value | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 1,000 | Manager |
| 1,001 - 5,000 | Department lead |
| 5,001 - 25,000 | Finance lead |
| Above 25,000 | Finance lead and CEO |

"Department lead" means the person at the top of that department's
branch in `general/org-chart.md` — Daniel for Engineering, Priya for
Growth, Tunde for People, Henrik for Finance.

## New vendors

New vendors need a completed vendor form, tax details, and a security
review if they will process customer data. See
`finance/vendor-payment-runbook.md` for the step-by-step execution of
this once approval clears — this document covers the approval matrix
only, not the setup mechanics.

## Contract terms

Default term is 12 months. Auto-renewal clauses must be flagged to
Finance before signing.

## Payment terms

Standard terms are net 30. Anything shorter needs Finance lead approval.

## Relationship to campaign and paid media approvals

This is the general procurement matrix. Growth spend specifically has its
own, sometimes stricter, rules layered on top:
`growth/campaign-process.md` (5,000 USD threshold) and
`growth/paid-media-policy.md` (2,000 USD/month per channel for paid
media specifically). Both ultimately route through this matrix once a
Finance approval is triggered, but the trigger threshold can be lower
than what this document alone would suggest.
